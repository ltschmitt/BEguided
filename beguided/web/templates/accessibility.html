Compliance status

Due to the following non-compliances and/or exemptions, this website is partially compliant with the requirements of the BfWebG in conjunction with BITV 2.0.

Non-accessible content

  • The Summary chart on the Results tab carries an aria-label but the underlying per-base-editor counts are not exposed in a tabular form to assistive technologies.
  • The dynamic data tables on the Results tab do not include <caption> elements. Section headings above each table provide context, but the tables themselves are not programmatically named.
  • The site is offered in light mode only. There is no automatic adaptation to the user's prefers-color-scheme setting.
  • The site offers no user-selectable customization: there is no theme switcher, font-size control, or high-contrast option. Dynamically rendered content (results table, sequence preview, base-editor profiles, toasts, summary chart) inherits the same fixed styling and cannot adapt to user preferences. This is a known technical limitation.
  • Hover tooltips on the Results tab are exposed only via the native HTML title attribute on the dynamically generated results table headers and on the corresponding filter labels in the results toolbar. Native browser tooltips disappear as soon as the mouse leaves the trigger element and are not keyboard-accessible, making them difficult to use for people who cannot keep the mouse steady over the trigger — including users with motor impairments, screen-reader users, and touch-device users.

We strive to remove barriers. However, due to technical limitations and resource constraints, we are currently unable to provide a fully accessible web presence.

3. Categories of personal data

  • Client metadata: IP address (salted SHA-256 fingerprint in logs only), user-agent string, request timestamp.
  • Uploaded content: the targets file you upload (chromosome, position, target_base, desired_base) or the raw sequences you paste. These may include genomic coordinates that, in combination with other data, can identify a person.
  • Generated results: the guide RNAs and editing predictions returned by the pipeline.
  • Custom base editor profiles: any custom XLSX or TSV you upload is held in memory only and discarded on server restart.

5. Recipients

Your data is processed exclusively on our own infrastructure. We do not transfer your data to third parties. The page loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or analytics beacons; Chart.js is served from the same origin with a Subresource Integrity hash.

The reverse proxy (Caddy) terminates TLS in front of the application and may log hashed client IPs. Logs are retained for at most {{ legal.log_retention_days }} days and then deleted.

6. Storage duration

Uploaded targets, generated guide results and job metadata are retained in the job database for at most {{ retention_hours }} hours after the job finishes and are then automatically deleted by a background sweeper. The revoke token associated with a job is stored only as a salted hash; the raw token is shown to you once at submission time.

You can request earlier deletion at any time by contacting {% if legal.contact_email %}{{ legal.contact_email }}{% else %}the controller{% endif %} and quoting your job ID.
